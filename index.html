Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Visit Help and support.

What is a smart link?

A smart link is one short URL that sends each visitor to a destination you configure for their device type.

How does device-based routing work?

When a visitor opens the link, SmartLink reads their device type and sends them to the matching destination: App Store, Google Play, your website, or your fallback. It routes to web or store pages and does not open an installed app.

Can one link support iOS, Android, and desktop?

Yes. Configure one destination per device type and a fallback for any device that cannot be identified.

Can destinations be changed after sharing?

Yes. Edit destinations at any time. The short URL and any QR codes stay the same.

Can I create a custom URL?

Yes. Custom slugs let you choose the ending of your link, subject to availability and your plan.

What is the difference between a custom slug and a custom domain?

A custom slug changes the ending of your link on SmartLink's domain. A custom domain uses your own domain name for the link, where your plan includes it.

Can I generate QR codes?

Yes. Create a QR code for any link and download it for print or packaging. A code is only scannable once it points to a valid link.

What analytics are available?

Clicks over time, device distribution and per-link totals. How far back you can see and what is included depends on your plan.

What happens when a device cannot be identified?

Visitors are sent to your fallback destination.

Can teams manage links together?

Yes, on plans that include team seats. Roles control who can view, edit or manage users.
